4x4 EXPERIMENT: MINIMAX vs. ALPHA-BETA
======================================

Settings
--------
Board size:            4x4
Marks in a row to win: 4
Search depth:          4
Position:              X (human) played row 2, column 2; O (AI) replied
Measured:              nodes explored by the AI for its reply

Measured results
----------------
Algorithm    Nodes explored    Move chosen
Minimax      35,715            row 4, column 4
AlphaBeta    3,556             row 4, column 1

Ratio: alpha-beta explored about 10% of the nodes minimax did
(roughly 10 times fewer).

Verification of the minimax count
---------------------------------
After X's first mark there are 15 free cells. No player can win within
4 plies, so minimax visits the whole tree down to depth 4:

15 + 15*14 + 15*14*13 + 15*14*13*12
= 15 + 210 + 2,730 + 32,760
= 35,715 nodes

This matches the measured value exactly.

Notes
-----
- The two algorithms chose different moves (row 4 column 4 vs. row 4
  column 1). This is expected: several moves have the same score, and
  both players pick randomly among the equally good ones.
- Both algorithms use the same evaluation function for positions that
  are not finished at the depth limit.
